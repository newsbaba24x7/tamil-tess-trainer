Benchmark results அளவீட்டு முடிவுகள்

Tests of tam_v3.1, the three open Tesseract models for printed Tamil (tam_best_v3.1, tam_v3.1 and tam_fast_v3.1), against Tesseract's stock Tamil models from tessdata_best, tessdata and tessdata_fast, and against tam_v3. No model here was trained on these test lines.

Results for tam_v3 and the models before it, on more test sets (197 whole pages, RANLP 2025, the Tamil OCR Benchmark, scanning resolution, handwriting and camera photos), are on the tam_v3 benchmark page. tam_v3.1 has not been run on those tests yet.

Summary

  • Real scanned lines: on tam_v3's 3,968 test lines from 197 pages, tam_best_v3.1 has a CER of 0.97% and a WER of 6.88%: 14% and 13% fewer errors than tam_v3 (1.13% / 7.92%), and 73% and 67% fewer than the stock model (3.56% / 21.16%). The integer tam_v3.1 scores 1.04% / 7.02% and tam_fast_v3.1 1.33% / 8.32%.
  • The gain over tam_v3 is significant: −0.16 CER points (−0.21 to −0.11) and −1.04 WER points (−1.38 to −0.71), 95% confidence intervals resampling whole pages.
  • Rendered lines in unseen fonts: tam_best_v3.1 is better than tam_v3 in every category of Tamil text, vowel signs, grantha, pulli, numerals, mixed Tamil-English and English terms. tam_fast_v3.1 is the best of all models on numerals, mixed text and English terms.
  • Quotation marks: 93.0% are read on the real lines (tam_v3 88.5%, stock 71.6%).
  • Still weak: Tamil numerals in tam_best_v3.1 and tam_v3.1 (57% read, stock 82%); Tamil symbols ௳–௺ ௐ; and word errors stay above 5%, mostly from punctuation and spacing.

Models compared

  • tam_best_v3.1 (float, Lfx192), tam_v3.1 (the same network in integers) and tam_fast_v3.1 (a smaller integer network, Lfx128), from release v3.1.0.
  • tam_v3 and tam_v3_fast (its integer version), from release v3.0.0.
  • Stock tam from Tesseract's tessdata_best, tessdata and tessdata_fast (4.1.0, trained in 2017).

Scoring

CER is the character error rate and WER the word error rate: lower is better. Text is compared after Unicode NFC normalisation with spaces collapsed. The main real-line columns use tam_v3's scoring: zero-width characters are dropped and quote and dash variants folded. The strict columns keep them, which counts the invisible U+200C that the stock models write after many pullis. Real lines are read one at a time with --psm 13, rendered lines with --psm 7. Confidence intervals come from a paired bootstrap with 10,000 resamples, resampling whole pages for real lines.

The two lines that stop tam_fast_v3.1 hit Tesseract bug #4627 (two unrecognised words in one line overflow a sum, and the command-line program traps the overflow). It is fixed in Tesseract 5.5.4 and does not affect page modes or programs that use the Tesseract library.

Reproduce these results

The scripts and the full report are in models/tam_v3.1 (RESULTS.md).

git clone https://github.com/newsbaba24x7/tamil-tess-trainer && cd tamil-tess-trainer/models/tam_v3.1
R=https://github.com/newsbaba24x7/tamil-tess-trainer/releases/download
for f in tam_best_v3.1 tam_v3.1 tam_fast_v3.1; do mkdir -p m/$f && curl -L -o m/$f/tam.traineddata $R/v3.1.0/$f.traineddata; done
curl -LO $R/v3.0.0/tam_v3-real-lines.jsonl.gz

# real test lines (downloads 197 page images from Wikimedia, 15 s apart)
python3 -I 14_fetch_real_lines.py tam_v3-real-lines.jsonl.gz --split test --out real_test
python3 -I 12_prepare_real_eval.py tam_v3-real-lines.jsonl.gz real_test/lines test
python3 -I 10_evaluate.py --lines real_test/lines --corpus real_test/lines/real.tsv --psm 13 --out results/real_test_psm13 \
    --model v31_best=m/tam_best_v3.1 --model v31_std=m/tam_v3.1 --model v31_fast=m/tam_fast_v3.1
python3 -I 18_report_tables.py results > results/tables.md

Add --model NAME=DIR for any other model (each DIR holds a tam.traineddata). The README in that folder has every step, including the rendered test lines, the check lines, the crash scan and the confidence intervals.
